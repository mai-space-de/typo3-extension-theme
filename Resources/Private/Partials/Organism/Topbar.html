Utility topbar — language switcher, search, and login. Sits above the sticky site-header. Scrolls away naturally (not sticky) so users on desktop see it at the top; it disappears on scroll and the main nav takes over as the single fixed landmark. Hidden on narrow viewports: those actions live inside the offcanvas panel (rendered by Menubar.html as .mai-menubar-offcanvas-footer).
